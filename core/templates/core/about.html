This is how our story begins

In2010, reado founder coby and his family moved 1500 miles away - and quikly realized video calls alone were not enough to keep his son and son's grandparents connected.

To bridge the distance coby turned to the power of stories. Combining beautifully digitized book with video chat technology, reado launched later that year 2012.

How does bookchat work?

Our patented BookChat platform makes it easier to capture kids’ attention. By combining interactive, digitized books with video chat, BookChat sessions last almost 10 minutes longer than traditional video calls.
